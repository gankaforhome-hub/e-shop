# Privacy Policy

This section describes the process of collecting, storing, using, sharing, and protecting your personal information when visiting the website and placing orders on the **GANKA FOR HOME** site. By visiting the website and/or placing orders, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

GANKA FOR HOME takes the protection of users' personal information very seriously and takes all necessary measures to ensure its security. Various technologies and procedures are applied to protect against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of information.

## Personal Information – Collection and Use

In the course of interacting with Users, GANKA FOR HOME collects the necessary information about Users in order to provide the highest level of service and to communicate with Users. Personal information can be changed or updated at the request of the User themselves. 

This information is not sold or shared with third parties under any circumstances (an exception can only be made by a court or arbitration decision).

## Delete Profile

To delete your profile, please send an email to [gankaforhome@gmail.com](mailto:gankaforhome@gmail.com) from the email address linked to your profile. We will process your request within three business days and delete your profile.

## Informing Users

The GANKA FOR HOME store can use phone, email, SMS notifications, and other communication channels to share information with its Users about existing products and services, promotions, special offers, contests, and other events. However, each such message includes an option that allows the User to stop or pause receiving these messages. 

User mailing lists are never sold or shared with third parties.

## Using Cookies

Cookies are pieces of information that a website sends to a User's hard drive to store information related to the site. Cookies technology does not contain personal information and cannot in any way configure the User's system or read information from their hard drive.

Users can disable cookies themselves in their browser settings. In that case, access to some functionality may be limited.

The site may include services from third-party companies (Instagram, Facebook, Google Analytics, etc.) that also use cookies. The store does not affect how they operate.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy is not final and may be changed or updated. Users will be informed of any changes to the Privacy Policy by email or a special notice on the website's main page.
