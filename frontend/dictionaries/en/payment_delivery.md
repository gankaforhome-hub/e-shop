# Payment & Delivery

How to pay for your order and how we deliver it across Ukraine and abroad. These
terms are part of the [Public Offer](/info/public-offer).

## Payment

Prices on the site are shown in Ukrainian hryvnias (UAH) and include all taxes
and duties required by law. The price of a product in a confirmed order does not
change.

You can pay for your order:

- online by bank card on the site when placing the order;
- cash on delivery when collecting the parcel at the carrier’s branch.

Online payment is available:

- by Visa or Mastercard on the site;
- via the Privat24 payment system;
- with Apple Pay or Google Pay.

The carrier’s cash-on-delivery fee is paid by the buyer at the carrier’s rates.
If a cash-on-delivery parcel is not collected within the carrier’s free storage
period, the order may be cancelled.

Personalised items are made only after 100% payment. We provide a payment
document (fiscal receipt) in paper or electronic form.

## Delivery

We deliver across Ukraine (except active combat zones) and internationally.
Delivery is free for orders over 5,000 UAH.

Delivery options:

- to a Nova Poshta branch;
- to a Nova Poshta parcel locker — available when paying by card on the site,
  for orders up to 15,000 UAH;
- by Nova Poshta courier.

Delivery time depends on how far away your town is. The production and dispatch
time for personalised items is agreed separately.

### Pickup

You can collect your order in person in Kyiv, 43 Antonovycha Street, Monday –
Friday, 11:00–19:00.

## Receiving your order

We pack every item with its fragility in mind, using protective packaging.

- Inspect the parcel and the product in the presence of the carrier’s employee
  before signing the waybill.
- If the product was damaged in transit, you may refuse to accept it. The damage
  is recorded in a report drawn up with the carrier’s employee.
- Please photograph the parcel and the product and let us know on the day of
  receipt.

The risk of accidental loss of or damage to the product passes to you when you
receive it.
