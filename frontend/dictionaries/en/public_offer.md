## 2. Terms and Definitions

In this Offer, the following terms have the meanings given below:

- **Product** — porcelain and ceramic items, decorative objects and related
  items under the GANKA FOR HOME trademark presented on the Site.
- **Art porcelain** — original works whose value lies primarily in the artistic
  image, subject and aesthetics. They are produced in limited editions and may
  be partially hand-painted or hand-finished.
- **Limited edition** — a series of items sharing one subject or design, the
  number of which is limited in advance. Once an edition is sold out, its
  reproduction is not guaranteed.
- **Personalised item** — an item made or decorated (engraving, inscription,
  image) to the Buyer’s individual order according to a design approved by the
  Buyer.
- **Buyer** — an individual who has placed an Order under the terms of this
  Offer.
- **Order** — the Buyer’s request to purchase selected Products, placed on the
  Site or by another method provided for in clause 5.1.
- **Acceptance** — the Buyer’s full and unconditional acceptance of the terms of
  this Offer in the manner set out in clause 5.4.
- **Carrier** — the delivery service used by the Seller to send the Product to
  the Buyer.

## 4. Features of Art Products

4.1. The Product is first and foremost a work of art and decor: its main value
lies in the artistic image, subject and aesthetic experience. The purpose of the
item and care recommendations (if any) are given in the Product description on
the Site.

4.2. Limited-edition items and items that are hand-painted or hand-finished are
unique. The Buyer agrees that such items have features inherent in handmade work
which are not defects of the Product, including:

- slight differences in the pattern, lines, brushstrokes, colour saturation and
  shades, both compared with the image on the Site and between items of the same
  edition;
- uneven layers of paint, glaze or decal, a perceptible relief of brushstrokes,
  brush marks;
- slight shifts of the image relative to the shape of the item, small dots or
  inclusions in the painting;
- natural features of the porcelain body and firing that do not affect the
  integrity of the item.

4.3. Product photographs on the Site are for illustrative purposes. Colour
rendering depends on the screen settings of the Buyer’s device.

4.4. The number of items in each edition is limited. If the Product sells out
before the Order is confirmed, the Seller notifies the Buyer. The Buyer may
choose another item or cancel the Order. In that case, any amount paid is
refunded within 7 calendar days.

4.5. Where stated in the Product description, the item is accompanied by a
certificate of authenticity or marked with its number in the edition.

4.6. Copyright in the images, subjects and design of the Product belongs to the
author or another rights holder. Purchasing an item does not transfer to the
Buyer any economic intellectual property rights in the images. Reproduction of
the images for commercial purposes without the rights holder’s written
permission is prohibited.

## 6. Price and Payment

6.1. Product prices are shown on the Site in Ukrainian hryvnias (UAH) and
include all taxes and duties required by law.

6.2. The Seller may change prices on the Site. The price of a Product in a
confirmed Order remains unchanged.

6.3. The Buyer may pay for an Order by one of the following methods:

- online by bank card on the Site when placing the Order;
- cash on delivery upon receipt of the Product at the Carrier’s branch.

6.4. Online payment is made:

- by Visa or Mastercard on the Site;
- via the Privat24 payment system;
- using Apple Pay or Google Pay.

6.5. The Carrier’s fee for cash on delivery is paid by the Buyer at the
Carrier’s rates, unless otherwise stated on the Site.

6.6. Personalised items are made only after 100% payment.

6.7. The Seller provides the Buyer with a payment document (fiscal receipt) in
paper or electronic form in accordance with the law.

6.8. If the Buyer does not collect a cash-on-delivery parcel within the
Carrier’s free storage period, the Seller may cancel the Order.

6.9. Refunds of amounts paid online are credited to the same card used for
payment. The crediting period depends on the Buyer’s bank.

## 7. Delivery and Acceptance of the Product

7.1. Delivery is carried out by the Carriers specified in the “Delivery” section
of the Site. Delivery within Ukraine is paid by the Seller, unless otherwise
stated on the Site or in the Order confirmation.

7.2. The production and dispatch time for Personalised items is agreed
separately.

7.3. The Buyer may collect the Product in person at: Kyiv, 43 Antonovycha
Street. Opening hours: Monday – Friday, 11:00–19:00.

7.4. The Seller packs the Product with due regard to its fragility, using
protective packaging.

7.5. Upon receipt, the Buyer inspects the parcel and the Product in the presence
of a Carrier’s employee before signing the waybill.

7.6. If the Product has been damaged in transit, the Buyer may refuse to accept
it. The damage is recorded in a report drawn up with the Carrier’s employee. The
Buyer is advised to photograph the parcel and the Product and to notify the
Seller on the day of receipt.

7.7. The risk of accidental loss of or damage to the Product passes to the Buyer
at the moment the Buyer receives the Product.

## 8. Return of Products of Proper Quality

8.1. The Buyer may withdraw from the purchase of a Product of proper quality
within 14 calendar days from the day of its receipt (Article 13 of the Law of
Ukraine “On Consumer Protection”).

8.2. A Product is accepted for return if it has not been used and its
presentation, consumer properties, packaging, labels and proof of purchase have
been preserved.

8.3. To return a Product, the Buyer notifies the Seller by email or via a
messenger (the form in Annex 1 may be used) and sends the Product via a Carrier
to the address specified by the Seller. The cost of returning the Product to the
Seller is borne by the Buyer.

8.4. The Buyer is responsible for packing the Product properly for return. A
Product damaged during return shipment due to improper packing is not considered
to have preserved its presentation.

8.5. The Seller refunds the full price of the Product to the Buyer within 30
calendar days from the day of receipt of the returned Product, to the card used
for payment or, for cash-on-delivery payments, to the account specified by the
Buyer.

8.6. Personalised items made to the Buyer’s individual order cannot be returned
or exchanged as products of proper quality (Article 13 of the Law of Ukraine “On
Consumer Protection”).

8.7. The features described in section 4 are not grounds for returning a Product
as defective, but do not limit the Buyer’s right to withdraw under clause 8.1.

## 9. Products of Improper Quality

9.1. A defect of the Product means a manufacturing defect that arose before the
Product was handed over to the Buyer (for example, a crack, chip or flaking of
the painting), as well as a Product that does not match the Order. The features
described in section 4 are not defects.

9.2. If the Buyer discovers a defect, they have the rights provided for in
Article 8 of the Law of Ukraine “On Consumer Protection”, in particular to
demand replacement with an identical Product (if available), a proportionate
price reduction or a refund.

9.3. To make a claim, the Buyer notifies the Seller and provides a photograph of
the defect. The Seller considers the claim within the time limits established by
law. The cost of shipping a defective Product is borne by the Seller.

9.4. The Seller is not liable for damage that occurred after the Buyer received
the Product as a result of a fall, impact or other mechanical action.
